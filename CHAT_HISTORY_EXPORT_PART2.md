**Sessional analysis** is the study of how liquidity, volatility, and orderflow rotate through the global 24-hour financial cycle as institutional money centers (banks, hedge funds, sovereign desks) open and close. 

In institutional trading (and specifically **ICT / SMC / Market Profile**), price does not move randomly across the day—it follows a repeating **algorithmic cycle of Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution (AMD)** tied directly to global banking hours.

---

### 1. The 3 Major Institutional Sessions

```
  00:00 UTC        07:00 UTC            12:00 UTC       16:00 UTC       21:00 UTC
┌────────────────┬────────────────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬─────────┐
│  ASIAN SESSION │    LONDON OPEN     │   NY OVERLAP  │  LONDON CLOSE │ DEAD/   │
│ (Accumulation) │   (Manipulation)   │ (Distribution)│  (Profit Take)│ ROLLOVER│
└────────────────┴────────────────────┴───────────────┴───────────────┴─────────┘
```

#### **A. Asian Session (Tokyo / Sydney / Hong Kong: ~00:00 – 06:00 UTC)**
* **Market Behavior**: Low volume, tight range, sideways consolidation.
* **Institutional Purpose (Accumulation)**: Market makers accumulate orders while retail traders place stop-losses just above the **Asian High** and below the **Asian Low**.
* **Key Assets**: JPY, AUD, NZD. On Gold (`XAUUSD`) and EUR/GBP, it typically establishes liquidity boundaries that will be hunted later.

#### **B. London Session (~07:00 – 12:00 UTC) — *The Highest Volume FX Hub***
* **Market Behavior**: Explosive expansion and volatility injection.
* **Institutional Purpose (Manipulation — The "Judas Swing")**:
  * Around **07:00 – 08:30 UTC**, algorithms engineer a false breakout past the Asian High or Asian Low.
  * This sweeps retail stop orders and triggers breakout traders into the wrong direction.
  * Once liquidity is filled, the smart money reverses price, frequently forming the **High of the Day (HOD)** or **Low of the Day (LOD)** (occurring ~70% of days in London).
* **Key Assets**: EUR, GBP, Gold, DAX.

#### **C. New York Session (~12:00 – 20:00 UTC)**
* **Market Behavior**: High-impact macro news drivers (CPI, PPI, NFP, FOMC at 12:30 / 14:00 UTC).
* **Two Dominant Regimes**:
  1. **London Continuation**: If London established a strong institutional trend, New York pulls back into a Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Order Block during the London/NY Overlap (12:00–16:00 UTC) and continues the trend.
  2. **NY Reversal**: If London hit an institutional Daily/H4 higher-timeframe HTF Key Level (S&D zone or Daily liquidity), NY creates a reversal back into the daily range.
* **Key Assets**: USD pairs, Gold, US Indices (NAS100, US30, S&P 500), Crypto.

---

### 2. Core Concepts & Models in Sessional Analysis

1. **The London Judas Swing**:
   * Asian Low swept $\rightarrow$ Reclaimed $\rightarrow$ Market Structure Shift (MSS) $\rightarrow$ Enter Long targeting Asian High + external liquidity.
2. **Session Initial Balance (IB) & Session Anchoring**:
   * In Candle Range Theory (CRT) and Market Profile, the high and low of the first 60 minutes of a session define the "Initial Balance". Breaking this range with volume predicts the trend for the remainder of the session.
3. **Session Killzones**:
   * Strict algorithmic execution windows:
     * **Asian Killzone**: 00:00 – 02:00 UTC
     * **London Open Killzone**: 07:00 – 10:00 UTC
     * **New York AM Killzone**: 12:00 – 15:00 UTC
     * **London Close Killzone**: 15:00 – 17:00 UTC (frequent counter-trend retracements as European desks close book positions)
4. **The "Dead Zone" / Asian Rollover (~21:00 – 23:00 UTC)**:
   * Broker spreads widen massively (spread blowout), liquidity evaporates, and volume drops to near zero. Algorithms should **never** take fresh entries during this period.
